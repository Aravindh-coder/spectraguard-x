VEYRAX 2.0 Proposed solution

SIH 2026
Real-world issue:

Microorganisms (Salmonella, E. coli, Aflatoxins) infect food processing lines silently, causing foodborne illness outbreaks, massive product recalls, and regulatory penalties.

Why important:

Traditional 24–48hr lab culturing lets infected batches reach consumer markets; manual spot checks miss patchy contamination, halting entire processing operations.

Solution:

VEYRAX fuses 256-band NIR Hyperspectral Imaging (900-1700nm), UV 365nm Fluorescence, RGB vision & ToF topography at the edge to detect microbial risk in <10ms and auto-reject contaminated products.

METHODOLOGY & PROCESS OF IMPLEMENTATION
1. Sense & Collect: NIR (900-1700nm), UV 365nm fluorescence, RGB spatial camera & ToF topography.
2. Edge Transmit: ESP32 streams telemetry packets over Wi-Fi / MQTT / OPC-UA.
3. Edge AI Fusion: Jetson Orin fuses 1D spectral + 2D spatial features into risk tensor.
4. Decide & Protect: Dual AI + Physics engine triggers microsecond pneumatic air-jet ejection.
5. Monitor & Learn: Live Digital Twin canvas, batch fingerprinting & active learning queue.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

SIH 2026
FEASIBILITY ANALYSIS
Technological Feasibility: Uses off-the-shelf edge sensors & AI. Retrofit-ready design clips over existing food conveyors without infrastructure shutdown.
Economic Feasibility: $250–$400 prototype cost vs $50,000 lab hyperspectral cameras, dramatically lowering adoption barriers for food MSMEs.
Operational Feasibility: Inline plug-and-play installation with zero line downtime. Intuitive SCADA twin requires minimal operator training.
Scalability: Modular sensor pod deployable across grain mills, dairy, fruit sorters, and meat processing facilities.
CHALLENGES AND RISKS
Harsh Environment: Steam, flour dust, humidity & oil splash affect optical window transmissive signal.
Data Imbalance: Novel or rare microbial mutations missing in training manifolds.
Connectivity Loss: Unstable factory Wi-Fi delaying cloud dashboard updates.
False Positives: Surface dust specks causing unnecessary food waste.
Safety Failures: Ejection failure leading to foodborne illness outbreaks.
STRATEGIES FOR OVERCOMING
Ruggedized Enclosure: IP65 sealed chassis with optical window residue detector & pneumatic air-knife wiper.
Open-Set AI: Out-of-Distribution Mahalanobis scoring flags unknown microbes as UNKNOWN ANOMALY — LAB VERIFY.
Offline-First Edge: Direct microsecond hardware relay trigger ensures sub-10ms rejection even offline.
AI + Physics Safety: Reference white tile self-calibration overrides AI if sensor conditions drift.
Closed-Loop Confirmation: Downstream optical sensor verifies physical ejection into isolation bin.
